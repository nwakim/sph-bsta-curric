Data Science Lifecycle

This diagram maps BSTA courses (and a few related non-BSTA courses) onto a standard data science lifecycle, based on a working draft curriculum-mapping exercise. Each course may not cover every stage — as students move from foundational to advanced courses, coverage of the lifecycle expands. The outer ring names cross-cutting practice dimensions (fairness, transparency, generalizability, sustainability, accountability) that apply across every stage.

Hover or tap a lifecycle stage (or its course badge) to see which courses cover it. Hover the outer ring labels for related notes. Red markers on the ring flag stages/transitions the draft identifies as possible curriculum gaps. This is a working draft, not an official curriculum document.
